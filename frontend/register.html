BusFlux Terms & Conditions

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering for an account on BusFlux, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions regarding bus reservations and travel. If you do not agree, please do not use our platform.

2. Bus Ticket Reservations

BusFlux acts as a ticketing platform. The respective bus operators provide the actual transportation service. Bus departure times, routes, and seat availability are subject to change by the operator.

3. Passenger Conduct

Passengers must behave in a respectful manner. Bus operators reserve the right to deny boarding to any passenger who is intoxicated, abusive, or poses a safety risk, without refund.

4. Luggage Policy

Each passenger is typically allowed one standard checked bag and one small carry-on. Excess or oversized luggage may incur additional fees directly from the bus operator at the time of boarding.

5. Cancellations & Refunds

Tickets can be canceled subject to the cancellation policy of the specific bus operator. BusFlux processing fees and platform charges are strictly non-refundable.

6. Platform Fees

A nominal convenience fee may be added to ticket purchases to maintain our platform services.

Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

We collect information such as your name, email, phone number, and booking history to provide you with a seamless bus booking experience.

2. Use of Information

Your details are shared with bus operators solely for the purpose of confirming reservations and verifying passenger manifests.

3. Payment Data

BusFlux does not store your credit card information. All payments are processed through secure, PCI-compliant third-party gateways.
